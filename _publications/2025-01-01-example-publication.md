We propose a dual-modality latent gloss alignment framework for gloss-free sign language translation. Our approach achieves fine-grained cross-modal alignment between frames and tokens by introducing latent gloss aggregation to consolidate features of the same category. The model achieves state-of-the-art results on two public benchmarks, significantly outperforming existing gloss-free methods.
