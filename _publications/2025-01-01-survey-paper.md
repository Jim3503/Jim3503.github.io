A comprehensive survey on sign language translation models, systematically analyzing technical architectures for spatio-temporal feature extraction, cross-modal alignment, and context decoding. This work covers the paradigm evolution from CNN-LSTM to multimodal LLMs from 2020-2025, and organizes the technical framework and open-source resources in the field.
